The chart installs the KiwixLibrary and ZimArchive CRDs. Create a library, then archives in its namespace:
  apiVersion: kiwix.jacaudi.dev/v1alpha1
  kind: KiwixLibrary
  metadata:
    name: library
  spec:
    storage:
      size: 100Gi

  apiVersion: kiwix.jacaudi.dev/v1alpha1
  kind: ZimArchive
  metadata:
    name: wikipedia-en-100
  spec:
    libraryRef:
      name: library
    source:
      download:
        catalog:
          name: wikipedia_en_100
          flavour: full
